Vendor Protection

Sell with confidence. Get paid, reliably.

Vetra Vendor Protection is the other half of the same escrow system that protects buyers: it's what guarantees a Vendor who ships a genuine order gets paid for it, on a predictable schedule, without chasing invoices.

How you get paid from escrow

The buyer's payment is already sitting in escrow before you even ship — here's how it reaches you.

Step 1

Buyer pays upfront

Payment is collected at checkout and held in Vetra's escrow — guaranteed funds for a confirmed order before you ever pack it.

Step 2

You fulfill the order

Ship within your listed handling time and log tracking details — this is your proof-of-fulfillment if a dispute ever comes up.

Step 3

Delivery is confirmed

The buyer confirms the order arrived — or, if they don't respond, it auto-confirms 48 hours after drop-off is logged on the tracking timeline.

Step 4

Funds move to your payout balance

Escrow releases the full order amount, minus the platform fee, into your Vendor balance the moment delivery is confirmed.

Payouts themselves run weekly for any order confirmed delivered at least 48 hours earlier, straight to the bank account on file — no waiting on invoices, no manual payout requests.

You're protected too, not just the buyer.

Escrow cuts both ways — it exists to make sure a legitimate sale actually pays out.

Guaranteed funds before you ship

Because payment sits in escrow before fulfillment, you're never shipping against a payment that could still fail or bounce.

Evidence-based dispute review

A buyer's dispute doesn't auto-refund. You're notified immediately and can submit tracking and delivery evidence before any decision is made.

Chargeback protection

Delivery-confirmed orders are backed by our own dispute process, so you're not left to fight a card-network chargeback alone.

Predictable weekly payouts

No waiting on invoices or chasing a buyer for payment — confirmed orders pay out on a fixed weekly schedule, automatically.

Storefront & logistics, built in

Storefront tools with no code required, plus logistics and delivery partners already in place — one less thing you have to source yourself.

Your brand, protected

Every seller is identity- and business-verified before listing, so buyers can't be tricked by a store impersonating yours.

If a buyer opens a dispute

How the review works

You're notified the moment a dispute opens and get 48 hours to respond with your side — tracking numbers, delivery confirmation, photos of the item before it shipped, whatever supports your case.

Vetra's resolution team reviews both sides and makes a call: funds release to you, a refund goes to the buyer, or — for a genuine partial issue — a partial resolution is worked out between both of you. If you disagree with the outcome, you can appeal it directly with our Vendor support team.

Orders where delivery was already confirmed and the payout window has passed are not reopened for a change-of-mind dispute — that protection is what makes the weekly payout schedule dependable in the first place.

What protects you

Proof of shipment and delivery tracking · listing photos and description on file · the 48-hour response window · an appeal path if you disagree with a ruling.

What we ask of you

Accurate listings and stock counts · shipping within your stated handling time · responding to disputes within the window · no counterfeit, restricted, or prohibited items.

Your payout details

Bank account details are stored securely and used only for payouts — Vetra never shares them with buyers or other Vendors, and fees are disclosed upfront on every order.
