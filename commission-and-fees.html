1. Vendor Commission

VETRA operates a commission-based marketplace. By registering as a vendor and listing products or services on the VETRA platform, the vendor acknowledges and agrees that VETRA is entitled to charge a commission on every successfully completed transaction facilitated through the platform.

The applicable commission rate shall be a uniform percentage of 3% to 5% across all product and service categories, unless otherwise communicated by VETRA.

2. Automatic Commission Deduction

The applicable commission shall be automatically deducted from the vendor's transaction proceeds before the remaining balance is released or settled to the vendor.

Vendors acknowledge and authorize VETRA to deduct the applicable commission from payments received for transactions conducted through the platform.

The vendor shall only be entitled to the net proceeds remaining after the applicable commission and any other clearly disclosed charges have been deducted.

3. Commission Transparency

VETRA is committed to maintaining transparency in all financial transactions.

The applicable commission rate shall be communicated to vendors before they commence selling on the platform. Vendors shall also have access to relevant transaction information showing the gross transaction amount, commission deducted and net amount payable.

No undisclosed commission or transaction charge shall be imposed on vendors.

4. Changes to Commission Rates

VETRA reserves the right to review, modify or adjust its commission rate when necessary for operational, commercial or business sustainability reasons.

Any proposed changes to the commission rate shall be communicated to affected vendors through appropriate communication channels and shall take effect only after reasonable advance notice.

Continued use of the VETRA platform after the effective date of a communicated change shall constitute acceptance of the revised commission structure.

5. Cancellations, Refunds and Reversed Transactions

Where a transaction is cancelled, reversed or refunded, the treatment of any commission associated with that transaction shall depend on the circumstances of the cancellation or refund.

Where a transaction is cancelled before successful completion and no commission has been earned, no commission shall be payable.

Where a completed transaction is subsequently refunded, VETRA shall review the circumstances and determine whether the commission should be reversed, refunded or retained, in accordance with its applicable refund and dispute resolution policies.

VETRA reserves the right to investigate suspected fraudulent transactions, unauthorized cancellations or attempts to manipulate the commission system.

Any commission adjustment shall be communicated to the affected vendor through the appropriate transaction or account records.
