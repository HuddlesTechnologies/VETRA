Why we exist

We built the middle,
so buying and selling could work.

VETRA is a modern African marketplace built around a simple belief: buying and selling should be easier, more accessible, and more trustworthy. We are creating a platform that brings vendors, businesses, and everyday buyers into one connected marketplace, giving people a better way to discover products, sell with confidence, and make informed purchasing decisions.

VETRA is designed for the realities of commerce in Africa. From the individual who wants to sell a product to a growing business looking for new customers, and from a buyer searching for a good deal to someone who simply wants a safer and more convenient way to shop, VETRA exists to make the marketplace work better for everyone.

VETRA (Vendors. Trust. Africa)

Our Purpose

VETRA exists to help people trade with greater confidence. We want to reduce the friction that often comes with buying and selling online — uncertainty, limited reach, fragmented marketplaces, and difficulty knowing who to trust. By bringing buyers and sellers together within one structured platform, we aim to make commerce more straightforward and dependable

A Marketplace for Both Buyers and Sellers

For buyers, VETRA is a place to discover products, compare choices, find vendors, and shop with greater confidence. For sellers, it is an opportunity to present their products, reach more customers, and participate in a growing digital marketplace without needing to build an entire e-commerce infrastructure from scratch. The platform is intended to accommodate both individual-to-individual commerce and business-to-consumer commerce. This hybrid approach gives VETRA the flexibility to serve the everyday marketplace while also supporting professional vendors and growing businesses.

What we hold ourselves to

These aren't posters on a wall, they're the rules the platform is built around.

Verify before you list

No seller goes live until their identity and business details are checked. No exceptions.

Hold funds until delivery

Payment only reaches a seller once a buyer has confirmed the order arrived as described.

Reviews from real orders only

You can only leave a review if you've actually completed a purchase. Nothing else gets published.

Resolve disputes fast

Order issues get a human response within hours, not tickets that disappear into a queue.

Pay sellers on a schedule

Weekly payouts, always, a small seller shouldn't have to chase invoices to get paid.

Build for every state

Logistics partners are chosen so a seller in Enugu can reach a buyer in Kano just as easily.
